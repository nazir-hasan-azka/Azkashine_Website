description: Template B — plan a new section or route, then wait for a go-ahead
argument-hint: [section name]

Plan the section named in $ARGUMENTS. **Write the plan and stop.** Do not create files.

Read first, and say what you found:

- `.claude/PLAN.md` for where this section sits and what shape it must not repeat.
- `lib/content/` for whether the copy exists. If it does not, that is part of the plan.
- The section above and below it, so the transition is designed rather than discovered.

The plan states:

1. **What it is** — one sentence a non-developer would recognise.
2. **The shape, and why it is not the shape above it.** The diagnosis of the old site was
   "six sections, one shape". A second grid needs an argument.
3. **Files** — each one, new or changed, with what changes.
4. **Content** — what comes from `lib/content/`, and what has to be written and cleared.
   Anything a deck does not evidence is flagged here, not written and flagged later.
5. **The transition** into the next section — ground colour, spacing, whether anything
   crosses the boundary.
6. **What could break** — name the standard most at risk and how you will check it.
7. **Open questions** — things only the user can answer. Ask them here, once.

Then wait.
